Anyone added on someone else's behalf now gets a way to sign in. Sharing a nest, a document or a folder with a new email, adding them to a team, or assigning them as a steward (in the app or over MCP) creates their account with a real temporary password and emails it to them with a sign-in link — the same email a Teammates invite sends. Previously only the Teammates invite did this; the other paths created an account nobody could sign in to until an admin reset its password.
