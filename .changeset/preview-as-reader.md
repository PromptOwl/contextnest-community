"Preview as reader" for nests with Reader Mode on. Owners and editors always saw the authoring view, so checking what visitors get meant a logged-out browser. The Reader Mode dialog now has a Preview as reader button that shows the visitor-facing reader in place — approved documents and approved text only, exactly as a visitor sees them — with an Exit preview bar to return to the authoring view. Rights are unchanged while previewing.
