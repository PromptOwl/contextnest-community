Sharing on an archived nest says it is archived. Opening a document's Share dialog in an archived nest showed the owner "Required permission: 'write', your permission: 'read'" — the archive cap in a permission's clothing — and offered a form that could only fail. Refusals on an archived nest now name the archive, and the dialog shows an archived notice with the add and remove controls gone; the existing roster stays visible for reference.
