Review queue: clicking a change now opens it in a pane beside the queue instead of a read-only popup. You can switch between Preview, Changes (a diff against the published version) and the published version. The author's note shows in full. Approve & publish takes one click, and Request changes takes its reason inline. Edit draft opens the edit-and-approve flow for reviewers who can edit. Each review has its own link (`?review=`), and after a decision the next change opens.
