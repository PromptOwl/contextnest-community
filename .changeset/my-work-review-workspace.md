My Work is a review workspace: the queue on the left and the document under review on the right, with Review, Sent back, Drafts and Mine queues, Preview / Changes / Published tabs, and the decision and Activity beside the text. "Select all up-to-date" leaves out reviews whose document has a newer version; `GET /me/work` and `context_my_work` report `up_to_date` on review items. Tasks, Mentions and Notifications are sections of the page.
