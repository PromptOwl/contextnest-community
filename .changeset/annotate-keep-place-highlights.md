**Switching views keeps your place, and comment highlights show in every view.** On a hosted artifact, flipping between Preview and Annotate used to reload the preview at the top and open the text at the top; both now land on the passage you were reading, the preview no longer reloads, and the commented passages are highlighted in both (click one to open its thread; click a thread's quote to go to it). On a document, switching between the editor, Comment mode and the markdown source keeps the same passage on screen, and comments are now highlighted in the editor and in the markdown source too, not only in Comment mode.
