Nest settings: a new **Systems** section lets an owner or admin manage named system keys from the app. You can create one (read or write, granted to this nest), copy its key (shown once), rotate or revoke it, and add or remove the nests you administer. In the collaborators list, a system shows a **System** badge and can't be made an admin.
