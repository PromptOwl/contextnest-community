**Quieter trial notice on content pages.** Inside a nest and in the document editor, the trial and renewal notice now shrinks to a single line (title, "Talk to us" link, dismiss); the full card stays on the dashboard. Dismissing it hides it for 30 days instead of a week. Urgent notices — the last few days before a change, or a lapsed license — still show in full everywhere and can't be dismissed.
