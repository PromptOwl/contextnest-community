Reader Mode is laid out as three cards that scroll on their own. The contents, article and "On this page" panels fill the window below the header and each scrolls internally, so the page never scrolls as a whole; the nest name heads the contents as a proper heading with a search box under it; the outline lists every heading of the open page, indented by level, exactly as the editor's outline does; Previous / Next are proper buttons after the article; and long nest, page and folder names truncate with a tooltip instead of stretching the layout.
