Make review decisions clearer and faster. My Work is now the one review queue, with a pane that shows who sent the change, a plain summary of what changed and a decision bar that stays on screen. Reviewers can send a change back with Request changes or close it with Reject, and the history records which one happened. A bulk bar approves a whole selection at once and names what it will approve. New nests start with Icebox, Prioritized, In process, In review and Done stages, and the board opens on them. Settings, Activity and Invite replace the old nest menu entries. The left rail is navy to match PromptOwl.
