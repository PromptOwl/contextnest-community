Editor says what saving does: "Publish" when the edit goes live, "Save draft" when it needs review (prime documents, governed nests). Submit for review now saves unsaved edits first ("Saved and submitted for review."), and edits to your own pending submission auto-save to it. Submit and Discard appear right after a save turns a published document into a draft, without a reload.
