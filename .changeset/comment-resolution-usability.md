Resolved comments now get out of the way. The comments rail lists open threads, with resolved ones folded under an expandable "Resolved (n)" section, and the header counts open threads only. Resolved threads no longer paint a highlight in the document, and the artifact viewer folds its resolved threads the same way. A new "Reply & resolve" button posts a reply and closes the thread in one step. Replying to a resolved thread reopens it, from the web UI or through MCP. The Review tab no longer lists comments from resolved threads. People already in a thread are notified when someone replies. When a thread's quoted text is no longer in the document, the thread shows a "text changed" label instead of pointing nowhere.
