Dashboard: opens on All nests and remembers whichever scope you last picked. Every card says whose it is: your own nests show "Yours", shared nests show "Shared by …", and nests you can read only because they are org or public show a dashed border with "Org-visible" or "Public". Clear filters goes back to All. The same marks carry into the nest: in the sidebar, nests shared with you get a people icon and org/public ones you weren't invited to are dimmed (hover names the owner and your role), and the nest page shows a banner with who shared it and your role.
