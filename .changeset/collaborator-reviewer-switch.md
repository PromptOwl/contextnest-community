Make someone a reviewer from the Collaborators tab. On a nest with stewardship on, owners and admins get a Reviewer switch on every person row, and an "Also make them a reviewer" checkbox when adding someone — so adding a user and letting them approve documents happens in one place instead of a trip to the Stewards tab. The switch manages a nest-wide reviewer steward; it is disabled (with a pointer to the Stewards tab) for someone who already holds a different nest-wide steward role, so it never silently replaces it. Tag- and document-scoped stewards are still managed on the Stewards tab.
