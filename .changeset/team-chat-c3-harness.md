Team chat harness, the screens (po-1200). With team chat on, each nest's Chat page is a three-pane harness: a sidebar with Channels (private ones marked), Direct messages with who is around, Saved and Mentions; the conversation in time order, grouped by author, with date lines, a "New" line, "Load older" and "Jump to latest"; the thread beside it with the nodes it touches. Messages take reactions, edits, deletes, pins and saves; a search box searches messages. "New message" starts a direct message or a group with people and agents; the header opens the members panel (who is around now, add people) and one menu for notifications (every message, mentions only, muted), join, leave, private or public, archive and restore. Someone typing shows under the composer, and everything updates live over the event stream with polling underneath. A launcher on every nest page opens the chat as a slide-over beside Hootie, with this nest's unread on it. Settings → Features has the "Team chat" switch; a nest's settings have its own switch and your default notification level there. A short explainer greets each person the first time. Direct and group messages are kept out of the document views.
