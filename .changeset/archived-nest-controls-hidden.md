An archived nest hides the controls that could only fail. In the nest view: Connect, the Reader Mode switch, Activity trace, and Assign / Remove / Edit steward. In a document: the visibility dropdown becomes a read-only badge, and Share, Share this document and Publish are gone. Everything read-only — version history, copy link, compare, comments — stays.
