Server settings are regrouped into tabs: **Basic** (community name, public base URL, logo, sign-in methods, product analytics, upload size, trace retention), **Features** (every on/off switch — workflow plane, sub-agents, PromptOwl teams import, content types, usage telemetry, single sign-on, agent SSO, nest creation, people directory) and **Hootie** (the chatbot with its model key). Single sign-on and agent SSO are switched on in Features; their configuration moves to a **Single sign-on** tab that appears only while one of them is on. New `community_name` setting (`COMMUNITY_NAME`), editable under Basic.
