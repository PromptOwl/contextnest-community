**Descriptive browser tab titles.** The tab title now names the page you're on and, inside a nest, the nest — for example "Board – QA Nest – ContextNest", "My Document – QA Nest – ContextNest" or "Teammates – ContextNest" — so several open tabs are easy to tell apart. A branded public reader keeps its own site title.
