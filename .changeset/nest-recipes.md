Recipes when you create a nest (PO-1411). The New nest dialog and the first-run welcome screen offer Product factory, GTM factory, Operations, Ideation or Blank. A recipe gives the nest its folders, starter documents (rulebooks, a thesis, CONTEXT.md, a glossary), a board grouped by stage with the recipe's columns (and the nest's task stages, so a new task lands in the first column), channels where team chat is on, its own distill ontology, and agents worth hiring, which show as agent recommendations (`recipe:<handle>`) until hired or dismissed. Then a "Connect a harness" step: Local (the existing Connect flow), Central lite and Central durable (docs only until their deploy bundle ships; see docs/central-harness.md). A recipe is a Surfaces seed manifest with new optional keys (`recipe`, `boards`, `channels`, `hire`, `distill`) applied by the seed engine, so re-applying is idempotent and a board, channel or stage set a person changed is kept. New: `GET /recipes`, `recipe` on `POST /nests` and `POST /onboarding/nest`, MCP `context_recipes`, and `recipe` on `nest_create` and `context_onboarding_update`. Migration 103 lets `surface_seed_items` record boards, channels and task stages.
