**Settings → License explains how seats are counted.** The Users and Editors rows each have an (i) with a tooltip: the current count against the plan, that each person counts once at their highest role on any nest, which roles make someone a user (license admin, superadmins, nest owners, admins, reviewers) or an editor, and that invited people with no role and viewers are free.
