New nest setting "Creators review their own documents" (`creator_is_reviewer`, off by default). In a governed nest, whoever creates a document becomes its reviewer steward and the first version publishes immediately; every later change — a teammate's or the creator's own — goes through review as usual, with separation of duties unchanged. Prime documents still start as drafts. Future creates only; nothing is stewarded retroactively.
