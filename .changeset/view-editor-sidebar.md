Views can now be created and edited in the app. A View's block list (node, list and callout blocks) is edited under its intro, with a preview that refreshes on save, and it appears in the sidebar's Views group. Other block kinds stay in place as locked rows. A node block that can't be shown now reads "This content is unavailable." for every cause, and `/health` reports `type_view_enabled`.
