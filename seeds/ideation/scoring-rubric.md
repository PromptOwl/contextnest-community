# Scoring rubric

Score every idea on the five questions below, 1 (low) to 5 (high). The score is the total, out of 25. Write the score and one line per question on the idea's page.

| Question | 1 | 5 |
|---|---|---|
| **Impact.** How much does it help the people it is for? | barely noticed | changes how they work |
| **Reach.** How many people does it help? | a few | most of them |
| **Confidence.** How sure are we it works? | a hunch | evidence in hand |
| **Ease.** How cheap is a first version? | months | days |
| **Fit.** How well does it serve the goal in the thesis? | off to the side | squarely on it |

## The weekly review
1. Look at the top five by score, plus anything a person wants to argue for.
2. For each: promote (it becomes work), park (with a date to look again), or drop (with the reason).
3. Record the outcome in reviews/ and each promotion in decisions/.
