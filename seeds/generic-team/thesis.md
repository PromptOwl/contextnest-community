# What this team is, and what is worth remembering

_Edit this page. Nest's listener reads it as its north star._

**Who we are.** One or two sentences: what the team does, for whom.

**Worth remembering.** Decisions and why they were made; who owns what; commitments with a date; durable facts about customers and projects; changes to how we work.

**Not worth remembering.** Status chatter, unanswered questions, ideas nobody agreed to, and anything about pay, health, performance or personal life.
