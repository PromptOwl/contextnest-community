# How this nest is organised

This nest is the team's shared memory. Nest proposes what is worth keeping from your conversations; a person approves it before it lands here.

- **decisions/**: agreed choices, one per document, with who decided and when.
- **people/**: who does what. Role, what they own, how to reach them at work, team. Nothing about pay, health, performance or personal life.
- **projects/**: one area per project or team, with its owner and current state.
- **customers/**: durable facts about customers and prospects: contract terms, renewals, contacts by role.
- **how-we-work/**: the way the team works, stated going forward.
- **agents/**: saved agents. Only nest admins can approve changes here.

Read **thesis** first: it says what this team is and what is worth remembering.
