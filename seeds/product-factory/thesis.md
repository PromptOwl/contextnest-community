# What this team builds, and what is worth remembering

_Edit this page. It tells people and agents what this product is for._

**What we build.** One or two sentences: the product, and who it is for.

**What good looks like.** The outcomes a feature must reach before it ships.

**Worth remembering.** Goals and the evidence behind them; design and PRD decisions and why; evaluation results; what a retro changed.

**Not worth remembering.** Status chatter, half-formed ideas nobody agreed to, and anything about pay, health or personal life.
