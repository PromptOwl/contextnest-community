# Standard: the product brief (stages 1 and 2)

**Stage 1, Intent,** turns a goal into a need: who has the problem, what they do today, what changes for them, and how we will know it worked.

A need states:
- the person and the situation, in their words where possible;
- the job they are trying to get done, and what gets in the way;
- MUST, SHOULD and WON'T, so scope is explicit;
- the success measure, and how it will be observed;
- what is assumed and not yet checked.

**Stage 2, Red team,** attacks the need before anything is designed:
- Who is left out, or harmed?
- Which assumption, if wrong, sinks the feature?
- What is the cheapest test that would prove it wrong?
- What already exists that does this?

The red team adds what it found to the need. It does not soften it.
