# Standard: design and design review (stages 3 and 4)

**Design** makes a prototype a person can click, not a description of one. It uses the product's own components, type and colours. It shows the main path, the empty state and one error.

**Design review** checks the prototype against the need, not against taste:
1. Can the person in the need finish the job without help?
2. Is every MUST visible? Is anything WON'T sneaking in?
3. Does each screen say plainly what it is and what to do next?
4. Does it work at phone width, by keyboard, and with a screen reader?
5. Is anything decorative that carries no meaning?

The review ends with exactly one line: `DESIGN_APPROVED: yes` or `DESIGN_APPROVED: no`. A no lists the concrete fixes, most important first.
