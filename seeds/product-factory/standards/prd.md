# Standard: the PRD (stage 5)

The PRD is what the build is judged against. It holds:

- **Summary:** the need in three lines, and a link to it.
- **Scope:** in, and explicitly out.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Given / When / Then, one behaviour each, numbered. Every MUST from the need has at least one.
- **Edge and error cases:** empty, too much, no permission, offline, slow.
- **Data and permissions:** what is stored, who can see it, what is logged.
- **Non-goals and open questions.**

Write criteria a tester can check without asking the author. No criterion, no feature.
