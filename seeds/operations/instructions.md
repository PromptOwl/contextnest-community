# How this nest is organised

This nest is a map of how work flows here. A good map lets anyone answer, without re-reading the pile of documents: what is the process, who owns each step, where does work stall, and what should we automate first.

- **processes/**: one document per recurring process: trigger, steps, owner, end state.
- **handoffs/**: where work passes between teams or systems, and what is passed.
- **systems/**: the tools and systems in use, and which processes touch them.
- **bottlenecks/**: where work waits, with how bad it is and the evidence.
- **automation/**: candidates to automate, ranked by impact and effort.
- **decisions/**: agreed changes to how we work, with who decided and why.
- **method/**: how this map is made, and the source and privacy rules.
- **agents/**: saved agents. Only nest admins can approve changes here.

Every improvement is a task on the **Improvements** board.

For agents: read method/how-to-map-operations first. Every claim carries its source and its grounding. New documents are drafts until a person approves them.
