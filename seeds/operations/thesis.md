# What this operation does, and what is worth remembering

_Edit this page. It tells people and agents what this map is for._

**What we run.** The operation in two sentences: what goes in, what comes out, for whom.

**The questions this map must answer.**
- What is our process for <the thing that matters most>, step by step, and who owns it?
- Where does work hand off, and where does it stall?
- Where are we most exposed: a single owner, a missing procedure, a fragile system?
- What should we automate first?

**Not worth remembering.** Secrets and credentials (never), personal details, and one-off exceptions that are not a process.
