# Who we sell to, and what is worth remembering

_Edit this page. It tells people and agents what this go-to-market is for._

**Who we sell to.** The buyer, their situation and the problem we solve for them.

**The funnel.** The stages a buyer moves through, from first exposure to a paid pilot, and the one number per stage we trust.

**Worth remembering.** Evidence with its source; hypotheses and what tested them; experiment verdicts; plays that work.

**Not worth remembering.** Vanity numbers we cannot trust, opinions with no evidence, and anything private about a person.
